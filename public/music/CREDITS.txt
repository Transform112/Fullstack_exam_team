How to add them (a human must do this before the demo):
1. Download one royalty-free track per name from https://pixabay.com/music/ or the
   YouTube Audio Library: soft-piano (gentle solo piano), happy-pop (upbeat light pop),
   party-beat (energetic dance), romantic-strings (slow strings).
2. Trim and compress each to at most 90 seconds:
   ffmpeg -i input.mp3 -t 90 -b:a 96k -ac 2 -af "afade=t=out:st=86:d=4" public/music/<name>.mp3
3. Record the source URL and license for each file below.
